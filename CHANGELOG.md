## 20.1.1 - 2026-07-12

### Added
  - Config option to stop specific entities from spawning with hats. Set `disabled_entities` in hats.toml to a list of full entity ids (e.g. "minecraft:zombie") or namespaces to exclude a whole mod (e.g. "minecraft").
